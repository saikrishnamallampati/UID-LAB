Tourism in India

Introduction to Tourism

Tourism is the activity of travelling to different places for enjoyment, relaxation, and cultural experience. It helps people explore new destinations and understand different traditions.

Popular Tourist Places

India is famous for its historical monuments, temples, beaches, and hill stations. Tourist places like the Taj Mahal, Goa, and Kerala attract visitors from all over the world.

More Information 

       Tourism plays an important role in the development of a country.
        It helps in improving the economy by creating employment opportunities and supporting local businesses.
        Tourism also promotes cultural exchange and allows people to learn about different traditions, heritage, and natural beauty. 
        By encouraging travel and exploration, tourism contributes to national growth and global understanding.
