AMRITAVISHWAVIDYAPEETHAM

         AMRITAAMMA,lovingly known as Amma, is a world-renowned spiritual leader from India.
         She is famous for her simple yet powerful message of love, compassion, and selfless service.
         Amma is best known for embracing people as a way of offering comfort, peace,
         and emotional healing, earning her the name “The Hugging Saint.”
